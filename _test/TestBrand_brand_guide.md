# TestBrand — Brand Guide

**Tone:** Professional, helpful, concise
**Primary color:** Blue (#2563EB)
**Secondary:** Gray (#6B7280)
**Audience:** Small business owners 25-55
**Voice:** Direct, no jargon. Speak like a trusted advisor.
**CTA style:** Imperative ("Download now", "Get started", "Try it free")
**Avoid:** Hyperbole, buzzwords, startup-pitch language
